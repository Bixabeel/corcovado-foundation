About Us

Conserving for Future Generations

The Corcovado Foundation drives initiatives that protect the extraordinary biodiversity of the Osa Peninsula through ecosystem conservation, community empowerment, and strategic partnerships.

For over three decades, we have been working to ensure that nature and people thrive together, promoting sustainable solutions that create a positive, lasting impact in one of the most biologically rich territories on the planet.

Support our mission Meet our team

Our approach

We are local activists dedicated to preserving and protecting Costa Rica's fragile biodiversity. We leverage environmental education to engage local communities and inspire them to take action. We support both governmental and non-governmental entities in their pursuit of sustainable, balanced, and equitable development. While our focus is deeply local, our impact is global—providing environmental education and empowering local communities, with a special emphasis on women, girls, and boys.

Corcovado Foundation: Turning Over Three Decades of Experience into Lasting Conservation

Our work is rooted in years of on-the-ground presence, building knowledge alongside local communities, scientists, allies, and regional stakeholders to develop conservation solutions that address the real needs of the Osa Peninsula.

Our History

The Corcovado Foundation was born out of a mission to support the protection of Corcovado National Park and preserve the extraordinary natural heritage of the Osa Peninsula. Over time, we have expanded our reach, developing programs in ecosystem restoration, environmental education, research, species conservation, and the strengthening of protected areas. Every project we undertake reflects a story of collaboration, learning, and deep commitment to this land.

Mission

To protect the ecosystems of Costa Rica's South Pacific by fostering sustainable development and empowering local communities through environmental education.

Vision

Empowered communities leading the conservation of their ecosystems, committed to sustainable development that continuously improves their quality of life and ensures the well-being of future generations.
