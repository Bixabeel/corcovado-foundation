Home/Programs/Ecosystem Restoration

Restoring ecosystems to strengthen biodiversity.

Ecological restoration allows us to recover degraded areas, strengthen connectivity between habitats, and promote natural regeneration through the planting of native species. This work contributes to improving ecosystem services and increasing landscape resilience to climate change.

Support restorationVolunteer with us

Why restore?

Healthy forests sustain biodiversity, regulate water, capture carbon, and benefit communities. Our program seeks to restore the ecological functioning of ecosystems through planned actions, technical monitoring, and collaborative work, so forests can regenerate and endure over time.

Native Reforestation

We focus 100% on native species. We have planted more than 4,000 species, respecting local ecology and improving biodiversity corridors.

Tree Monitoring

Annual measurements are taken of the growth and biometrics of planted trees, survival rates, surroundings, vigor, and foliage.

Fauna Monitoring

We use camera-trap technology to monitor wildlife and generate conservation data, which has allowed us to detect a 400% increase in wildlife presence in the reforested area.

Restoration begins with an action.

Every contribution allows us to bring resources to the field to recover forests, strengthen biodiversity, and build ecosystems capable of regenerating and enduring.
